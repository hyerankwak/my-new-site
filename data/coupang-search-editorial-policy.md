## Selection And Writing

- Check existing published and queued pages for the same intent first.
- Prefer a specific buying decision supported by a broader queried topic.
- Confirm the exact product, edition, accessories vs full game, manufacturer
  instructions, and stated age before attaching an API affiliate link.
- Never automatically choose the first search result or a promoted unrelated
  product. API rank is not an independent recommendation or a sales ranking.
- Include who the product suits, who should skip it, concrete use examples,
  limitations, edition differences, and a useful decision table.
- Keep product facts distinct from editorial suggestions and practice rules.
- Put the affiliate disclosure before the first commercial card. Place a
  reviewed card after the introductory answer, and a relevant final card
  after the decision guidance; do not add excessive duplicate buttons.
- Use API-provided product images, not review photos, for exact product cards.
- Use two distinct existing suitable generated body images with the site's
  full generated-image disclosure; do not depict those as product evidence.
- No fabricated ownership, testing, awards, savings, ratings, review counts,
  scarcity, popularity, developmental outcomes, or income guarantees.
- Where price cannot be refreshed reliably, link to the actual option price
  instead of freezing an old API price in a scheduled article.
- Keep sponsored/nofollow/noopener attributes and descriptive button labels.

## Publishing And Evaluation

Keep current scheduled articles unchanged until a reviewed replacement
batch is explicitly prepared. This policy does not modify scheduled tasks.
Publish prewritten, reviewed HTML through the existing deployment pipeline.
Verify mobile/desktop layout, decoded images, canonical/schema, index links,
sitemap, live HTTP status, and actual title before reporting success.

Assess Search Console impressions/clicks and Coupang report performance at
the granularity the account really supports. Do not claim per-article
sales attribution without an established supported attribution mechanism.
Improve existing pages with impressions but weak click-through before
creating near-duplicate recommendation pages. Do not promise rankings or
affiliate revenue based on query volume alone.
